sort1 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know?: It runs very quickly on already sorted input but becomes much slower on reversed and random input, which is characteristic behavior of bubble sort.

sort2 uses: Merge Sort

How do you know?: It consistently runs much faster than the other programs on large inputs and performs similarly regardless of input order, which matches the O(n log n) behavior of merge sort.

sort3 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know?: Its performance is relatively slow and does not significantly improve with sorted input, which is typical of selection sort’s O(n²) time complexity.
